Our Story

Built on a simple belief.

Hangar & Harbor was founded on a simple belief: the assets people are proudest of — their car, their boat, their aircraft — deserve to be cared for with the same precision and discretion, regardless of where they're kept.

Too often, that care is split across a car detailer, a marine cleaning crew, and an aviation contractor, each with a different standard. We built Hangar & Harbor to close that gap — one house, one standard, across land, sea, and sky.

We are a young company and proud of it. Every engagement is treated as an opportunity to prove the standard we've set for ourselves, by hand and by appointment.
